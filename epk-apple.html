About the Artist

Crafted from
contrast and silence.

Born from the intersection of ambient electronica and cinematic orchestration, NOIR ECLIPSE emerged in the underground scene of 2018 and has since redefined what contemporary instrumental music can be. With a sound rooted in minimalist tension and maximal emotion, each release is a deliberate act of contrast.

Drawing from classical training and a restless curiosity for sound design, the project fuses organic textures with synthetic architecture. Live shows are immersive audio-visual installations — audiences don't just attend, they inhabit the work.

With four studio albums, a Mercury Prize nomination, and stages from Berlin's Tresor to Coachella's main stage, NOIR ECLIPSE stands at the frontier of modern electronic music.
